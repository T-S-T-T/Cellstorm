Visual: will apply to both player ans enemies
    the swarm will no londer be a bunch of dots clumping together. a swarm will be 1 entity with a soft ball object physics. the size of the soft ball is fixed for the player and for enemies: it does not change with HP or cell count. enemy size is set by its type. the class "giant" doubles the player's size.
    the swarm hitbox will be slightly larger than the softball visual
    using weapons that require cells (gun, jaw, etc...):
        every weapon that requires cells needs 100 cells every time, no matter the HP or body size. the class "giant" increases this to 200 cells. the size of the weapon is proportional to body size.
        the swarm will spawn the needed cells to form the weapon
        the spawned cells will take x% of max health(weapon will not work if current HP < x%). the taken HP will be shared among the spawned cells. cells will also take x% HP regen. cell will have the same DEF as swarm
            by default, x = 10. x can be increased or decreased to + or - dmg
        when using the weapon, if current HP goes below the threshold of the used weapon, it will stop deploying. this should have the same effect as releasing the attack button
        when using the weapon, each cell spawned will have its own HP, DEF. the spd is determined by weapon deploy time. cell spd is ALWAYS faster than swarm spd
        the moment a weapon is deployed, the required amount of cells are spawned inside the swarm. the cells then quickly move to their own spots
        the cell's HP is linked to the swarm own HP. when a cell is damaged, the dmg is reflected on the HP bar
        if a cell dies, a new cell spawns. the cells then reorganize to make the shape again.
        when a cell spawns, it belongs to the cell group. more cells will only spawn if the deploying weapon needs more cells. if more cells are present during deploying, the excess cells moves back to the swarm then despawn
            this is to prevent spam clicking and spawn infinite cells
        when the weapon finish firing, all of the cells move back to the swarm
        Cell regen. Do the cells take x% of regen and heal themselves, while the body’s regen drops by x% while deployed?
            No. the swarm will keep the same regen.

        What does changing x do?
            in certain upgrades, x will be reduced and dmg will increase (by %)
                for now, make an upgrade to increase the dmg by 50% and increase x to 30%
        only 1 weapon of this type can be deployed at a time (for this version, ram does NOT count as this type, so for example, ram and gun can be used at the same time, but not gun and jaw)
